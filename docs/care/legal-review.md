# Care legal and regulatory review checklist

This checklist prepares counsel and the provider's compliance owner; it is not legal advice and engineering cannot approve it.

- Confirm the product's role, contracts, business-associate obligations, privacy notice, breach process and subcontractors.
- Approve retention, legal hold, deletion, backup and subject/guardian access rules for each record class.
- Review consent, electronic signature, audit, identity proofing and guardian/delegate authority.
- Validate Tennessee program, payer, licensing, incident/reporting, EVV, medication, staffing, training, claims and record requirements for the pilot's actual services.
- Confirm that Patvero never represents internal workflow states as submission to a regulator, MCO, clearinghouse or payer without a real receipt/acknowledgement.
- Review accessibility obligations and the evidence supporting any conformance statement.
- Approve marketing language; remove unsupported “HIPAA compliant,” “certified,” “approved,” “guaranteed,” or equivalent claims.

Current Tennessee workflows can change. The operational owner should validate launch procedures against the [Tennessee DDA Reportable Event Management page](https://www.tn.gov/disability-and-aging/provider-information/reportable-event-management.html) and applicable TennCare/MCO materials immediately before pilot. As of August 2026, the state page says REM moved to PERLSS on March 16, 2026; Patvero must not imply that an internal incident status submitted anything to PERLSS.
